# FIGURE 1 | How does untreated LD differ from non-LD controls?

**Synthetic demonstration. No experimental measurements or tissue-derived biological findings are shown.** The comparison is **LD SALINE vs NON-LD control**, with six simulated mice per group. All mice are on the LDLR−/− background. The original synthetic simulation is retained without effect-size rescaling; it is not fitted to brain tissue and does not predict experimental effects or power. The demo log-scale model is unsuitable for production effect estimation, and a real-data analysis pipeline has not been implemented or tested here. Positive log2 fold change and positive normalized enrichment score (NES) refer to the LD SALINE end of the comparison.

**a,** Volcano plot of 7,647 retained synthetic genes. Yellow and blue identify higher and lower expression in LD SALINE, respectively, at **adjusted *P* < 0.05 and absolute estimated log2 fold change ≥ 0.2**. Other genes are gray. Dashed lines mark adjusted *P* = 0.05 and log2-fold-change cutoffs of −0.2 and 0.2; zero is a direction reference. The joint criteria select 1,826 genes in this simulation: 936 higher and 890 lower. The [simulation summary](synthetic_demo/data/simulation_summary.json) records these counts separately from FDR-only counts. These are synthetic illustration results, not study findings or power predictions. Fitted effects are unchanged and not clipped at the cutoffs.

**b,** Expression heatmap of the 40 genes with the smallest adjusted *P* values among those passing both panel-a criteria, restricted to the 12 LD SALINE and NON-LD control samples. Rows show gene-wise z-scores of log2-normalized expression across these samples (sample standard deviation, ddof = 1), ordered by average-linkage Euclidean clustering. The scale runs from blue through white to yellow, limited to −2 to 2. Row standardization shows patterns rather than absolute effect magnitudes. Columns are individual mice. The LD SALINE annotation strip is yellow and the NON-LD control strip is blue; these are comparison-specific encodings, rather than fixed cohort colors.

**c, KEGG preranked GSEA; d, GO Biological Process preranked GSEA.** Both use all 7,647 retained genes ranked by signed t statistic, without either DEG filter. Each panel shows the two largest positive and two most negative NES values, regardless of significance. Marker color indicates direction (yellow, positive; blue, negative). Filled markers denote empirical GSEA FDR q < 0.05; hollow markers denote q ≥ 0.05. The q column reports GSEA empirical FDR, not Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted *P*. Pathway memberships come from real native-mouse annotations, but SIM identifiers are assigned to mouse symbols by a fixed random one-to-one mapping independent of the statistics. The enrichment is genuinely computed but carries no tissue-derived biological interpretation, including when a q value is small. The display rule does not select pathways to support a mechanism.
