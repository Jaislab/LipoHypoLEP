# Historical experimental data: figure legends

These figures use the supplied differential-expression results from `DGElist_AnkeData(1).xlsx`. They are exploratory analyses of historical experimental data, distinct from the synthetic template. The source statistical model, shrinkage procedure and original signed test statistics were not supplied. All tested genes were exported, as confirmed by the user. The workbook contains no individual sample-expression values or replicate metadata.

## Figure 1

**How does untreated lipodystrophy differ from non-LD controls?** Hypothalamic differential-expression results compare saline-treated aP2-nSrebp1; LDLR−/− mice (**LD SALINE**) with non-lipodystrophic LDLR−/− mice (**NON-LD control**), using the `saline_VS_wt` tab. Positive effects refer to LD SALINE. **a**, Volcano plot of all 15,518 tested genes. Yellow and blue indicate higher and lower genes, respectively, meeting adjusted P < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change ≥ 0.2: 142 higher and 432 lower genes. **b**, Supplied point estimates for up to three genes per direction with the smallest adjusted P values among those joint-cutoff hits. **c,d**, Exploratory preranked GSEA for KEGG and GO Biological Process, respectively. Positive NES indicates enrichment toward genes higher in LD SALINE. The common display and enrichment methods below apply.

## Figure 2

**What does leptin treatment change in LD mice?** Hypothalamic differential-expression results compare leptin-treated with saline-treated aP2-nSrebp1; LDLR−/− mice (**LD LEPTIN vs LD SALINE**), using the `saline_VS_leptin` tab with fold-change signs reversed. Positive effects refer to LD LEPTIN; supplied P values, adjusted P values and SEs are unchanged. **a**, Volcano plot of all 15,136 tested genes. Tan and turquoise indicate higher and lower genes meeting both cutoffs: 512 higher and 238 lower. **b**, Selected supplied point estimates, chosen by the same rule as Figure 1. **c,d**, Exploratory KEGG and GO Biological Process GSEA. Positive NES indicates enrichment toward genes higher after leptin treatment. The common methods below apply.

## Figure 3

**What differences remain after leptin treatment?** Hypothalamic differential-expression results compare leptin-treated aP2-nSrebp1; LDLR−/− mice with non-lipodystrophic LDLR−/− mice (**LD LEPTIN vs NON-LD control**), using the `leptin_VS_wt` tab. Positive effects refer to LD LEPTIN. **a**, Volcano plot of all 19,721 tested genes. Orange and purple encode higher and lower effects meeting both cutoffs. **b**, The single eligible gene, **Tcaf2**, with supplied log2 fold change 3.459 and adjusted P approximately 3.17 × 10⁻¹³. No lower genes meet both criteria. **c,d**, Exploratory KEGG and GO Biological Process GSEA. Positive NES indicates enrichment toward genes higher in LD LEPTIN.

The supplied post-treatment effects are unusually concentrated near zero, and effect/SE ratios do not recreate the source P values. Upstream effect estimation and shrinkage need clarification before effect magnitudes are compared across contrasts. This comparison alone cannot distinguish persistent LD-associated effects from treatment-induced effects. A nonsignificant difference does not establish normalization or equivalence.

## Common display and analysis methods

All gene-level plots retain the supplied estimates. Dashed volcano lines mark log2 fold changes −0.2 and +0.2 and adjusted P = 0.05. Genes meeting both criteria are colored; all others are gray. The estimate cutoff is a display/selection rule following the supplied zero-null test, not a statistical test of effects exceeding 0.2. Volcano axes are shared across the three figures. Panel b has explicit per-contrast symmetric effect axes; the estimates are unchanged. Dashed vertical lines mark zero and the effect cutoffs. Gene selection uses adjusted P, then Ensembl ID to break ties, with up to three higher and three lower hits. No confidence intervals are reconstructed from the supplied SEs, whose estimation procedure is unknown. No expression values or biological replicates are invented.

For GSEA, the primary score is **sign(oriented supplied log2 fold change) × −log10(source raw P)**. This is a derived proxy, not the missing original test statistic. Every tested Ensembl row in each tab remains in its ranking and background; neither DEG cutoff filters enrichment. A separate sensitivity run replaces the score magnitude with the two-sided normal-equivalent Z magnitude, preserving direction and ranking order while changing weighted enrichment. Full results for both metrics are retained.

Native-mouse gene sets are from the pinned archived KEGG BioSystems 2020 collection and GO BP annotation projection dated 19 May 2026, with the documented upstream protein-coding, evidence and size restrictions. Exact case-preserved workbook symbols map memberships to measured Ensembl IDs only when globally unambiguous across the workbook. Ambiguous symbol memberships are excluded; their measured gene rows remain in every applicable background. No random identifier assignment, maximum-statistic duplicate selection or unvalidated alias conversion is used. Thirteen legacy mitochondrial labels have KEGG coverage but no exact modern GO symbol match; this specific GO gap limits mitochondrial interpretation.

Weighted preranked GSEA uses weight 1, 1,000 gene-set permutations, seed 20261020, two threads and intersected set sizes 15–500. Empirical GSEA FDR q values are calculated separately for each contrast, collection and score metric. They are distinct from gene-level BH adjusted P values. A displayed q of 0 preserves the finite-permutation estimator's output; it does not mean an exactly zero false-discovery probability, and is not replaced by an unsupported numerical bound. Gene-set permutations do not preserve pathway gene correlation or estimate uncertainty across biological replicates.

Panels c and d display up to the two largest positive and two most negative NES values. If a direction has fewer than two sets, remaining positions use the largest absolute NES among unselected sets. Selection does not require significance. Filled markers indicate q < 0.05; open markers indicate q ≥ 0.05. Numeric labels are GSEA FDR q values, with threshold-aware formatting. Pathway names wrap without reducing their type size. Positive NES and positive gene effects always refer to the first named group.

Every text element uses 32 px Nimbus Sans on a 1536 × 1024 canvas, including panel letters, axes, ticks, gene labels, pathway labels and q values. Group names and the scientific questions appear here and in the repository gallery. The artwork contains analytical labels without methods or interpretive commentary.
